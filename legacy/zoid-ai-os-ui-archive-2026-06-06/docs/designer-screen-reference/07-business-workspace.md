# Business Workspace Screens

## Business Dashboard
Components:
- Header: Search Business, New Contact, New Company, New Follow-up, Settings
- Summary cards: follow-ups due, Gmail threads, active companies, business tasks
- Contacts preview
- Companies preview
- Follow-ups list
- Linked emails
- Business notes / SOPs
- Recent history
- Right inspector

## Contacts
Components:
- Search/filter/sort toolbar
- Contacts list: name, company, role, email, phone, tags, follow-up, activity
- Contact preview inspector
- Contact detail tabs: Overview, Emails, Tasks, Notes, Products, Follow-ups, History
Actions: Open, Email, New Follow-up, Add Note, Create Task, Link Product, Delete.

## Companies / Clients
Components:
- Search/filter toolbar
- Company list: name, status, contacts, tasks, follow-ups, products, activity
- Company preview inspector
- Company detail tabs: Overview, Contacts, Emails, Tasks, Notes, Products, Follow-ups, History
Actions: New Contact, Draft Email, New Follow-up, Add Note, Create Task, Link Product, Archive/Delete.

## Follow-ups
Components:
- Search/filter toolbar
- Follow-up list: title, contact/company, due date, status, linked email/task, notes
- Follow-up inspector
Actions: Open, Mark Done, Snooze, Draft Email, Create Calendar Event, Link Email, Convert to Task.
